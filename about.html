About EDC

Built by educators, for the schools and teachers of Thailand.

Our Story

A recruitment partner rooted in the classroom.

EDC was founded to close the gap between qualified English teachers abroad and the Thai schools that need them — with a process that respects both sides: transparent for applicants, thorough for schools.

Today EDC supports partner campuses across Bangkok, Chiang Mai and Phuket, managing everything from first application to visa paperwork and first-day orientation.

Mission

Match the right teacher to the right classroom.

We screen for teaching fit, not just qualifications on paper.

Vision

A trusted staffing standard across Thai education.

Recognized by schools and teachers alike as the clearest way to hire and be hired.

Values

Transparency, integrity, and follow-through.

Every applicant knows exactly where they stand, at every stage.
